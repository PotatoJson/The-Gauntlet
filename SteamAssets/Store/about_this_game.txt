SHORT DESCRIPTION (plain text only)
------------------------------------
Fight your way down with nothing but your fists. Hellbent is a third-person action RPG where the gauntlets you wear and the gems you socket decide how you fight, and one death ends the run.


ABOUT THIS GAME
------------------------------------
[Insert 01_fireball_charge.png]

Hellbent is a third-person action RPG about punching your way through whatever stands in your path.

Clear each chamber, keep moving deeper, and make your potions last. If you die, your run is over.


BUILD YOUR GAUNTLETS

[Insert 02_gem_socketing.png]

Your build lives on your hands. Wear a different gauntlet on each hand, socket gems into both, and merge matching gems to make them stronger.


CHOOSE YOUR ELEMENT

[Insert 03_choose_a_gauntlet.png]

Every gauntlet you find is a choice. Each element changes how you fight, so experiment and find the style that suits you.


FIGHT WITH WEIGHT

[Insert 04_cave_ambush.png]

Throw light attacks, charge up heavy ones, then roll out of danger. Enemies come at you together, and each one asks for something different.


FACE WHAT WAITS BELOW

[Insert 06_hell_approach.png]

The deeper you go, the worse it gets. Not everyone down there will go down easily.


FEATURES

- Elemental gauntlets with their own combat styles
- Gems to socket and merge into a build of your own
- Permadeath runs, with an auto-save after each cleared chamber so you can quit and resume
